Privacy Policy

Home Assistant YouTube is a private application used for personal home automation.

The application may access Google and YouTube data that the user explicitly authorises through Google OAuth.

The data is used only to provide the application's Home Assistant functionality, including retrieving YouTube video information and selecting videos for playback.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for marketing.

Authentication credentials and authorised Google data are used only for the operation of the user's private Home Assistant system.

The application is not offered as a public service.
